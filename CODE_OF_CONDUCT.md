# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

TaoPulse is committed to fostering an open, collaborative, and respectful
community. We welcome contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels and
strive to create an environment where everyone can participate constructively.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

* Be respectful and professional in discussions and reviews.
* Provide constructive feedback and criticism.
* Focus on ideas, code quality, and project goals rather than individuals.
* Assume good intent when interacting with other community members.
* Help maintain a welcoming and productive environment.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

* Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
* Offensive, abusive, or threatening language.
* Trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith participation.
* Sharing private or confidential information without permission.
* Any conduct that negatively impacts the safety or productivity of the
  community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct and may
take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior, including:

* Requesting changes in behavior.
* Removing comments, issues, or pull requests.
* Temporarily or permanently restricting participation.

Enforcement decisions will be made at the discretion of the maintainers and in
the best interest of the project and its community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please
report it privately to the maintainer through either of these channels:

* Element / Matrix direct message: [`@cogina.solutions:matrix.org`](https://matrix.to/#/@cogina.solutions:matrix.org)
* Email: [owldevs@proton.me](mailto:owldevs@proton.me)

Non-sensitive concerns may also be raised through
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/cogniax/tao-pulse-app/issues) or
[Discussions](https://github.com/cogniax/tao-pulse-app/discussions).

All reports will be reviewed promptly, and maintainers will respect the privacy
of the reporter.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the
[GitHub repository](https://github.com/cogniax/tao-pulse-app) (issues, pull
requests, and discussions), the Element / Matrix community room, and other
project-related interactions. It also applies when an individual is officially
representing TaoPulse in public spaces.

By participating in this project, you agree to abide by this Code of Conduct.
